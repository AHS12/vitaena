# Changelog

What's new in each version.

## 0.1.0 (November 15, 2026)

**A first look at the desktop app**

- Vitaena is now a desktop app. Your CV is laid out as real pages while you type, and the
  PDF you export is exactly what the preview shows, however many pages it runs to.
- PDFs read cleanly in applicant tracking systems: real text, contact details kept whole,
  each job's dates beside the job.
- Saving to a library and to files, autosave, undo, backups, updates and a tour come along.
