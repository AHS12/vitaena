# Changelog

What's new in each version.

## 0.2.4 (November 23, 2026)

**CVs for the country you're applying in**

- Choose where a CV is for in For, on Home or in Style: the United States, Canada, the UK
  and Ireland, Australia and New Zealand, Europe (Europass), Germany, Austria and
  Switzerland, France and southern Europe, the Netherlands and the Nordics, the Gulf, South
  Asia, or anywhere.
- Choosing a place sets the paper and how dates are written, and puts the templates that
  suit it first, each saying so. A new CV also gets the sections CVs there usually have,
  such as personal details for Germany, or nationality, visa status and driving licence
  for the Gulf. Undo takes it back, and your photo is never touched.
- Check tells you what employers there expect, and why: no photo or date of birth in the
  US, at most two pages in Germany, a European level for each language for Europass. It
  hides the photo or changes the paper in one click.
- Write dates as Jan 2020, 01/2020, 01.2020 or 2020, in Style, Dates.

**Three new templates**

- Lebenslauf, for German, Austrian and Swiss employers: every section as a table, with
  dates in a left column and a passport-style photo top right.
- European, laid out like a Europass CV, with languages as a grid of the five European
  skills.
- Harvard, the plain one-page resume US career offices recommend.

**Place, date and signature**

- End a CV the way German and South Asian CVs end: the place and date, such as
  "Berlin, 19.11.2026", and a picture of your signature under it, or a line to sign on.
  Turn it on at the end of Content. Every template has it.

**Language levels from A1 to C2**

- Give your languages European levels, for all five skills at once or one by one, or mark
  one as your mother tongue. Every template writes them, as "German (B2)".

**Ten more languages**

- Write a CV or biodata in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish,
  Turkish, Russian or Ukrainian. Their fonts come with the app, so there's nothing to
  download.
- Import reads CVs in these languages too, Europass language grids and a closing place and
  date among them.

**Fixes**

- Changing the language of a biodata's second labels now changes every label Vitaena
  wrote, not only the empty ones. Labels you typed stay.

## 0.2.3 (November 20, 2026)

**Write it in your own language**

- Write a CV or biodata in Bangla, Hindi, Marathi, Urdu, Arabic, Persian, Japanese,
  Chinese (simplified or traditional) or Korean, as well as English. Choose it in Write in
  on Home, or in Style, Language.
- Vitaena writes its own headings, labels, relations, months, dates and "Present" in that
  language, and the comma between items too. Headings and words you wrote yourself stay as
  they are.
- Urdu, Arabic and Persian run right to left, and two-column designs turn around to suit.
- Numbers can be in the language's own digits, such as ০১২৩, or in 0 to 9.
- Every design draws every language, and the PDF and Word file carry the right fonts.
- Import a CV or biodata written in any of these languages from a PDF or a Word file.

**Fonts for every language**

- Several fonts come with the app for Bangla, Hindi and Marathi, and for Urdu, Arabic and
  Persian. Choose one in Style, Font, or leave Matching to suit your main font.
- Fonts for Chinese, Japanese and Korean are larger, so they don't come with the app.
  When you choose one of these languages, Vitaena offers to download its fonts once,
  from 11 to 29 MB, and every document in it then uses them. Settings, Storage lists them,
  to download or remove.
- Add your own font, a .ttf or .otf file, in Style or in Settings, Storage, and use it in
  any design. It's kept with your documents and goes into backups. An older Bijoy font,
  such as SutonnyMJ, isn't added, since it stores Bangla as English letters.

**Biodata in two languages**

- Turn on Two languages in Style to write each heading, label and relation again under
  the first, smaller, in English or another language, as many printed biodata are.

**Fixes**

- When an assistant changed a document twice very quickly, the copy kept from before the
  first change could be lost. Each one is now kept.

## 0.2.0 (November 16, 2026)

**Use it with your AI assistant**

- Let an AI assistant you already use read, check and change your CVs and biodata through
  Vitaena: Claude, Cursor or Copilot, an open-source one such as OpenCode, Cline or Goose,
  or a model running on your own computer in an app such as LM Studio. Vitaena itself
  still has no AI.
- It's off until you allow it, in Settings, AI assistants, as Read only or Read and edit.
  Nothing outside your computer can reach your documents.
- An assistant can look at your pages, run Check, make a new CV or biodata, copy one to fit
  a job, change the content and the design, export any format, and import a CV from a PDF
  or Word file.
- A change to the document you have open arrives unsaved, as a step you can undo, with a
  message saying which assistant made it.
- Before an assistant changes a saved document, a copy is kept, and Settings lists what
  each assistant did, with Put back.
- Settings gives the lines to paste into each assistant, with Vitaena's place on your
  computer filled in. Two ready-made requests, Fit a CV to a job and Review a CV or
  biodata, appear where your assistant offers them.

## 0.1.9 (November 16, 2026)

**Start from the CV you have**

- Import a CV from a PDF or a Word file, and Vitaena makes it into one you can edit: your
  name and contact details, each job and degree with its dates, your bullets, skills,
  languages and photo.
- It works on CVs made in Word, Google Docs and online makers, in one column or two. It's
  all read on your computer, with no AI and nothing sent anywhere.
- A biodata comes in as a biodata, with your heading line, your details, your date of
  birth and height, and your family.
- Anything it can't place waits in a hidden Not sorted section, and Check reminds you to
  sort it.
- Find Import a CV on Home, or open a PDF or Word file with Open a file.

**Biodata**

- Make a marriage biodata as well as a CV: your details, your family and your photos, on
  one or two pages to share.
- Three templates: Heritage, traditional with a border; Clean, with no ornament; and
  Portrait, led by your photos.
- Write your own line above your name, and add a small picture of your choosing.
- Show your date of birth as the date, your age or both, and your height in feet and
  inches, centimetres or both.
- Add ready sets of labels: Horoscope, Religious practice, Lifestyle and More about me.
- Check asks for what families look for first, and says when the text would be too small
  in a shared picture.
- Share it as a PDF, a picture of each page, or a Word file.

**CVs**

- Add Personal details, or any rows of a label and a value, to a CV.
- Home has a tab for CVs and resumes, and one for biodata.
- Your documents shows CVs and biodata together, each marked with its type, or one type
  at a time.

**Fixes on the Mac**

- Templates, fonts, sizes and margins in the design pane respond to clicks again.
- Menus close when you click elsewhere.
- The preview no longer shrinks and grows when a scrollbar appears.
- Check works again.

## 0.1.5 (November 15, 2026)

**Vitaena on Linux**

- Install it on Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, Arch and others from a .deb, .rpm or Arch
  package, or run the AppImage on any distribution from 2024 or later.
- The AppImage updates itself. With a package, Vitaena tells you a new version is out and
  links to it.

**Six new templates**

- Professional: a bold name, contact icons and a round photo, with skills and projects
  in a side column.
- Academic: dates in the margin and publications as a numbered list, for a full research
  and teaching record.
- Executive, Bold, Timeline and Sidebar: centred and formal, a band of colour across the
  top, jobs along a timeline, and a full-height coloured column.
- Each template says whether it suits a resume, an academic CV or either.
- Modern's side column has more room around its edges.

**Photos and levels**

- Add a photo. Templates that suit one show it, and Show on the CV hides it without
  removing it.
- Show a level beside languages or skills: turn on Show levels in a group and click the
  dots, in half steps if you like. The word beside it, such as Native, fills in for you.
- Draw levels as dots, a bar or squares from Style, or leave it to the template.

**A roomier editor**

- Content and Check sit on the left, the page in the middle, and Design on the right, with
  its own Templates and Style tabs.
- Every template shows your own CV, or an example while yours is still empty.
- Template pictures appear quickly, with a placeholder while they're drawn, and the page
  you're editing always comes first.

**A new Home**

- Your recent CVs come first, then ways to start, including any template, then every CV
  with a search.
- See all, beside Recent, opens the page of every CV.

**Screening systems**

- Two-column templates have a Two columns or One column (ATS) switch above the page.
- Check's warning about columns turns on ATS mode in one click, and Check tells you when a
  level has no word a screening system can read.
- Export offers a one-column PDF for online forms beside the usual one, without changing
  your design.

**Export**

- One Export button opens a menu of every format, with a line on what each is for.
- Export a Vitaena file, to move a CV to another computer or keep a copy.

## 0.1.0 (November 15, 2026)

**Vitaena is now a desktop app**

- Your CV is laid out as real pages while you type, and the PDF you export is exactly what
  the preview shows, however many pages it runs to.
- Everything stays on your computer. No account, no cloud and no tracking.

**Write it your way**

- Drag sections, jobs, bullets and skills into the order you want, or move them with
  Alt+Up and Alt+Down.
- Pick months from a calendar, type skills as chips, and press Enter for the next bullet.
- Make words bold or italic with Ctrl+B and Ctrl+I, and add links.
- Keep a detail but leave it off the page with Show on the CV.

**Make it look right**

- Four templates: Classic, Modern, Minimal and Compact, each shown with your own CV.
- Five fonts, any colour, and your choice of text size, margins, spacing and paper.

**Send it with confidence**

- Check shows what a screening system reads from your PDF, with plain advice on what
  might trip it up.
- Export a PDF in one click, or a Word file or plain text for forms that ask for them.
- Open a CV you exported from the old web version of Vitaena and carry on.
